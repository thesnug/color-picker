## Findings and decisions

Written by hand after the run; the sections below it are generated. Every
label set here is a draft written by an agent and not yet reviewed by Jill, so
these decisions should be revisited once she has.

**Committed:** `DESCRIBE_COLOR_ACCEPT` 0.35 (new) and `PLAUSIBILITY_THRESHOLD`
0.4 → 0.8. **Kept:** `MOOD_WEIGHT` 0.4. No question wording was changed, so no
question version was bumped.

- **Recolor plausibility at 0.4 flagged nothing.** Jev's Noul answers for swaps
  ran from 0.49 to 0.93, so every labeled implausible swap (an indigo
  strawberry, a blue pumpkin, a scarlet lemon) passed. 0.8 catches 9 of 10 and
  wrongly flags one (a Hermosa Pink strawberry, 0.58). It sits close to several
  plausible swaps (0.80, 0.83, 0.84), so it is sensitive to a small shift in
  Jev's answers. Leaning high is deliberate: a wrongly dropped plan gives its
  slot to the next garment, while a wrongly kept one ships an implausible print.
- **Pattern, recolor wording.** The three confident wrong answers (scarlet
  strawberry leaves 0.76, a blue pumpkin 0.81, pink lemon leaves 0.75) are all
  cases Jev can call "intentionally stylized". The question and the `true`
  criterion both offer stylization as a way to pass, which likely compresses
  every answer upward. **Proposal:** drop "or intentionally stylized" from
  `swapQuestion` and the `true` criterion, and let the state's `mood` carry
  style (a neon rave design can still accept anything); bump
  `VET_RECOLOR_VERSION` to 2 and rerun. Not made here, because the wording was
  fixed in INT-2270 and is Jill's call.
- **Words to color at 0.35** is the lowest cutoff with precision at least 0.9
  (0.92, recall 0.88). Both errors it still accepts are arguably label gaps
  rather than wrong answers: "old terracotta flowerpot" → Brick Red and "fresh
  spring leaves" → Green. If Jill adds them to `expected`, precision at 0.35
  becomes 1.0.
- **Pattern, words-to-color wording.** Jev's misses lean to the plainest name in
  the family (Red, Green, Brown, Brick Red) over the more specific color the
  phrase points to. **Proposal:** add to the instructions "prefer the color
  whose hue and lightness fit the description best, not the plainest name";
  bump `DESCRIBE_COLOR_VERSION` and rerun. Not made here, for the same reason.
- **Mood weight: direction, not a decision.** Mean reciprocal rank rises with
  weight, from 0.43 at 0 to 0.63 at 1.0. But three of eleven designs have none
  of the picks in the deterministic shortlist, so the re-rank cannot reach them,
  and the picks are an agent's taste, not Jill's. Weight 1.0 would also discard
  the contrast order within the shortlist. Kept at 0.4 until Jill reviews the
  picks; if the direction holds on her labels, 0.6 to 0.7 is the likely move.
- **Mood discriminates weakly.** Most picks scored _complements_ and most
  dark-garment picks _neutral_; Jev rarely used _clashes_ or _elevates_. If
  Jill's picks show the same flatness, the level descriptions may need sharper
  contrast between the middle two.
- **Caveat.** The recolor evaluation sends all of a design's labeled swaps in
  one request, including several targets for the same element. Production
  sends one plan's swaps, one target per element. Questions are answered
  independently, but the grouping is not identical to production.
